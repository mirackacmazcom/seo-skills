# Patents by category (326 families)

Each entry is one patent family. Notes are based on the independent claims (Claim n) or on a description paragraph ([¶n]); entries without a note carry an excerpt from the patent's own abstract. For details: `scripts/search.py show <number>`.

### 1. Local result ranking — core (relevance, distance, prominence)

- **[US7822751B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7822751B2/en)** — *Scoring local search results based on location prominence* (priority 2005, granted)  
  Local results are given a query-independent **location prominence** score (Claim 1). The description gives example inputs to the score: the score of the 'authoritative' (official) document for the business [¶56], the number of documents containing reviews of the business [¶59], and the number of information documents that give address/phone/hours (such as Citysearch, Zagat) [¶60]. The document most often cited as the patent counterpart of Google's official 'relevance, distance, prominence' trio.
- **[US8312010B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8312010B1/en)** — *Local business ranking using mapping information* (priority 2007, granted)  
  For each business listing, user actions are counted by type: business-type queries, map requests for the address, actions on the map; a **request to contact the business (e.g. a call) is weighted more heavily than the others**, and the weighted total is used in ranking (Claim 1).
- **[US8122013B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8122013B1/en)** — *Title based local search ranking* (priority 2006, granted)  
  The **title** of the local listing (the business name) is sent to a web search engine as a query; a 'webscore' is derived from the number of results returned and used in local ranking (Claim 1). In other words, the business's general prominence on the web is carried over into local ranking.
- **[US8538973B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8538973B1/en)** — *Directions-based ranking of places returned by local search queries* (priority 2010, granted)  
  Ranks places with a score that combines **distance** to the user's location with the **number of past directions queries** made to that place (Claim 1).
- **[US7792883B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7792883B2/en)** — *Viewport-relative scoring for location search queries* (priority 2006, granted)  
  In location search, results are scored by the proximity of the geographic feature to the user's **map viewport** (Claim 1).
- **[US8463774B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8463774B1/en)** — *Universal scores for location search queries* (priority 2008, granted)  
  In a location query, geocoding results are ranked with an 'address support score': universal scores that measure how well the query elements are supported by address components (Claim 1).
- **[US8484199B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8484199B1/en)** — *Ranking of geographic information* (priority 2006, granted)  
  A **different ranking function is selected according to the type** of geographic feature, and a score is produced from the feature's physical attributes (area, population, etc.) (Claim 1).
- **[US8407211B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8407211B1/en)** — *Determining relevance scores for locations* (priority 2010, granted)  
  **Category-location relevance scores** are stored for each location; these scores are derived from the category relevance scores of the entities at that location (Claim 1).
- **[US7606798B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7606798B2/en)** — *Methods and systems for improving a search ranking using location awareness* (priority 2003, granted)  
  A **distance adjustment factor is determined according to the topic** of the query; for each document a topical score is combined with a distance score (Claim 1). In other words, distance matters more for some topics and less for others.
- **[US9262541B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9262541B2/en)** — *Distance based search ranking demotion* (priority 2013, granted)  
  The score of a distant local document is **demoted** if there is another local document within a threshold distance of the user (Claim 1). The distance penalty is conditional. Continuation in the same family: US11893034B2.
