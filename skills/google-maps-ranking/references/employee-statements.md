38. **Christina Wichiencharoen** (PM, Maps), 2023-11-22 — Rating is temporarily turned off during sudden waves of 1-star reviews. https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/how-google-maps-protects-against-fake-content/ — primary
39. **Ian Leader** (Director of PM, UGC), 2025-04-07 — A Gemini-powered model catches suspicious profile edits (e.g. a cafe switching to the plumber category); in 2024, 12 million+ fake profiles and 240 million+ reviews were removed. https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/google-business-profiles-ai-fake-reviews/ — primary
