### A) Pages that officially explain ranking factors

1. **Tips to improve your local ranking on Google** — https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091 — ✔ — **The core source.**
   - "Relevance is how well a Business Profile matches what someone is searching for."
   - "Distance refers to how far each business is from the customer who's searching."
   - "Prominence means how well-known a business is." On prominence: "…how many websites link to your business and how many reviews you have."
   - "More reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking."
   - "There's no way to request or pay for a better local ranking on Google."
   - **Changes to the page:** The summary sentence now says "popularity" ("Local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and popularity"); "Prominence" remains in the heading and the definition. On 14–15 July 2025 the page was shortened and the sentence "Your position in web results is also a factor, so search engine optimization (SEO) best practices apply." was removed. In addition, the phrase "links, articles, and directories" was narrowed to link and review counts (secondary: https://www.seroundtable.com/google-local-ranking-docs-updates-39761.html).
2. **Search for nearby places & explore the area** (Maps Help) — https://support.google.com/maps/answer/4610185 — ✔
   - "Local search results in Maps are based primarily on relevance, distance, and prominence." It says a business that is farther away may be shown higher if it is more likely to have what is being searched for. Payment does not affect Maps results.
3. **Search locations on Google Maps** — https://support.google.com/maps/answer/3092445 — ✔

### G) Quality Rater Guidelines: local sections (version 2025-09-11)

PDF: https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/guidelines.raterhub.com/en//searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf — ✔a. These are instructions given to raters, not ranking factors.
- §12.3 Locale and User Location (p. 96)
- §12.4 Queries with an Explicit Location (p. 97): "When there is an explicit location in the query, pay attention to it!"
- §12.7.4 Visit-in-Person Queries and User Location (pp. 105–107)
- §16.1 Queries with Both Website and Visit-in-Person Intent (pp. 152–154)
- §22.0 Rating Visit-in-Person Intent Queries (p. 164): results at or near the user's location are the most helpful ones; what counts as near varies by type of business.

**Not found:** No official page was found for the ranking of "Things to do" or for the ranking criteria of free local product listings. No official help page was found that explains the "Most relevant" ordering of reviews in Maps or the current status of the "Your match" score ("Your match" is explained only in the 2018 blog posts). Official pages name the factors but give no weights.
