## Accounts and things to watch

- **John Mueller:** X @JohnMu (effectively moved to Bluesky after mid-2024), Bluesky johnmu.com, Mastodon
  @johnmu@mastodon.social, LinkedIn johnmu, Reddit u/johnmu, Stack Exchange (Webmasters/Pro Webmasters), Hacker News
  johnmu, GitHub softplus, site johnmu.com. Most of the old Google Webmaster Central forum and Google+ content has
  been deleted from the internet.
- **Gary Illyes:** X @methode, Mastodon @methode, Bluesky methode.bsky.social, LinkedIn garyillyes (since December
  2022; where he is most active). His writing and talks are often exaggerated and humorous; always read what he says
  in context.
- **Martin Splitt:** X @g33konaut (rarely used), Mastodon @geekonaut, Bluesky divingfor.fun, GitHub AVGP (also has
  open source work outside Google; do not present comments unrelated to SEO as SEO statements), Hacker News avgp.
- **Danny Sullivan:** @searchliaison (X, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads) is a Google **role account**. Posts after
  1 August 2025 may belong to someone else; the header shows this note. His personal accounts are @dannysullivan (X)
  and dannysullivan.bsky.social (mostly non-SEO). Before joining Google (before 2017) he was the founder of Search
  Engine Land and a journalist: his statements from before 2017 were made **as a journalist, not as a Google
  employee**.
- **Daniel Waisberg:** X @danielwaisberg; there are also his pre-Google analytics writings (Online Behavior). Check
  the date of a statement to say whether it belongs to his Google period.
- **Rajan Patel:** X @rajanpatel. Mostly product announcements.

## Transcript speakers

- In Search Off the Record transcripts speaker names are written out and mapped to the person: "John", "Gary",
  "Martin", "Lizzi", "Danny", "Daniel", "Cherry" and their full names. Guests (e.g. Dave Smart, Barry Schwartz,
  Aleyda Solis, other Google employees) are not mapped to a person; `(speaker-labelled)` does not appear in the
  header for them.
- YouTube videos and external podcasts were transcribed without speaker separation. Participants were taken from the
  title/description. Work out who said something from the surrounding text with `show --at`.
- Name matching covers only these eight people. If another "Danny" or "Martin" speaks (rare), the person in the
  header may be wrong; check the episode description.
